Research

SONIC · Read-only transactions without replication-critical designs

first authorunder review2025 – present

Proves that "replication-critical" read-only transaction designs are unnecessary (the SONIC and Remote-Read theorems), then demonstrates it in two systems: Starry, built on the University of Washington's TAPIR codebase in C++, and COPS-SNOW extended with chain replication. Both are validated under crashes and partitions with Jepsen falsifier suites I wrote, and I contributed fixes upstream to Jepsen along the way.

TxJBOF · Concurrency control and write-path offload for DPU + JBOF storage

lead studentin progress2026 – present

Storage built from a DPU and a shelf of NVMe SSDs puts every write through the DPU's weak CPU. I reproduced Scalio (OSDI '25) end to end on CloudLab with RDMA NICs, SPDK and NVMe-oF, root-causing two artifact issues on the way, and designed a client-side write path that takes the DPU CPU off each write: clients write to the SSDs directly over NVMe-oF and coordinate through one-sided RDMA. Evaluated on emulated NVMe so far.

  • C++
  • RDMA

  • DPU

Juicer · Lower tail latency for strict serializability, without touching the database

co-authorin submission2025 – present

A transparent layer in gRPC-Go interceptors that reorders in-flight transaction messages by timestamp before they reach the data shards. It cuts P99 latency by up to 75% and aborts by up to 85% for strictly serializable databases with zero database-code changes, and beats NCC (SOSP '23) by 54% on tail latency. I implemented the concurrency-control protocols (2PL wound-wait and wait-die, MVTO) in its Go transactional key-value testbed and built the workload generator.

  • Go
  • gRPC
  • 2PL / OCC / MVTO
  • tail latency

EaaS · Evaluation-as-a-Service for distributed storage systems

top contributor2023 – 2025

An end-to-end framework for evaluating distributed storage systems correctly and reproducibly. I built its workload engine (YCSB, transactional YCSB-T, interactive TPC-C) and the adapters for CockroachDB, Cassandra and EPaxos-family protocols. Our cross-region AWS measurements refuted EPaxos's published 3× throughput claim (1–1.5× measured) and uncovered an EPaxos livelock at 9 replicas.

Nervos Fiber · Rust payment-channel network on CKB

2 fixes merged upstream2026

I wrote a Jepsen suite for Fiber (process kills, pauses and network partitions, with invariant checks on channel state and balances) and used it to reproduce a channel deadlock. Across 6,000 randomized fault schedules the deadlock occurred 1,030 times before a root-cause fix I proposed and 0 times after; the maintainers opted for a protocol redesign instead of merging it. Separately, two of my fixes are merged upstream, one of them for a reconnect bug I found and reported.

authorMIT2026

A deterministic stand-in for a broker API that a real agent CLI runs against, with historical price replay, injected timeouts with late commits, and a checker that catches orders filled twice. It applies the Jepsen idea to LLM agents: inject the fault, then check the invariant.
